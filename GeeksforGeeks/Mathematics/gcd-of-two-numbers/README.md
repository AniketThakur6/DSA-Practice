## [GCD of Two](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/gcd-of-two-numbers3459/1?utm_medium=article_practice_tab&utm_campaign=article_practice_tab&utm_source=geeksforgeeks)

**Difficulty:** Basic  
**Topics:** Mathematics  

**Problem Description:**

<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given two positive integers <strong>a</strong> and <strong>b</strong>, find <strong><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/greatest-common-divisor-gcd/">GCD</a> </strong>of a and b.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Note:</strong> Don't use the inbuilt gcd function</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> a = 20, b = 28
<strong>Output:</strong> 4
<strong>Explanation:</strong> GCD of 20 and 28 is 4</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> a = 60, b = 36
<strong>Output:</strong> 12
<strong>Explanation:</strong> GCD of 60 and 36 is 12</span></pre>

**Expected Complexities:**

Time Complexity: O(log(min(a, b)))  
Auxiliary Space: O(1)
